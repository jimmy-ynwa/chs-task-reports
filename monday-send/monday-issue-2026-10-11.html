  1. Sends Monday at 7 AM, the same clock Thursday runs on.
  2. Goes to the people who voted yes for the first six Mondays. Everyone who didn't say no joins after that. A reader who voted no never gets it.
  3. You make the picks, the pack does the rest. Each Friday it proposes three ranked-list topics with shortlists from your own data. You choose and type your five picks. It never picks for you.
  4. No news on Monday. If something breaks, Monday points to Thursday in one line.
  5. Same brand, trimmed template. A copy of LATEST Less Code with the Thursday-only blocks removed.
  6. Your cap is 45 minutes a week. If it needs more, the issue gets smaller.

Part 2 · The pack

The weekly clock

A scheduled cloud task builds Monday the same way Wednesday builds Thursday. No Mac, no pay-as-you-go billing. It all runs inside your Claude subscription.

Fri morningPack drops three ranked-list topics, with shortlists, into a Todoist task.
Sat to Sun noonYou pick, type five picks and your one-liners. Optional: a number, a poll question. Tick Ready.
Sun 4 PMCloud run builds the whole draft and a report page.
Sun eveningYou read the report, tweak it, schedule it. About 15 minutes.
Mon 7 AMIt sends.
Next SundayStats have settled. Numbers get logged next to Thursday's.

Your time: about 20 minutes on the picks, 10 on the number, 5 on the poll, 10 to 15 on review. That fits the 45-minute cap.

What the run does, step by step

0

Check the gate

Reads the Monday row for that week. If Ready is unticked, it changes nothing and reports that it's waiting. That's exactly how Wednesday works, and you can kick it off by hand any time.

The only required input is your five picks. Everything else has a fallback.
1

Build the Week Ahead

Pulls Monday through Sunday from the Events table, applies your filters, ranks what's left, and writes one-line descriptions using the same description rules the Thursday pack uses. Monday to Thursday in full, weekend as teasers.

Rule: no confirmed start time, no listing. Same as Thursday.
2

Build Monday Music

Pulls Monday to Thursday from the Live Music table, removes cancelled shows and repeats, then picks two a day with genre variety.

3

Write the ranked list

Takes your five picks and your one-liners exactly as typed. It drafts the headline, the two-sentence intro and the subject line, and scores them against the 13% Playbook so it knows how hard the hook is.

It never invents an opinion. If a one-liner is missing, that line is left blank and flagged.
4

Write Monday Math, the poll and the Dinner Club line

Writes up your number from its source. Builds this week's poll in Beehiiv and fetches last week's results. Finds the next open Dinner Club date.

5

Fill the partner slot and assemble

Fills the single sponsor slot from the Placements table or deletes it. Builds the draft from a new Monday template, checks the size so Gmail never clips it (target: under 60% of the limit; Thursday runs 86 to 91%), and checks that no placeholder text survived.

6

Log it and report

Adds a Monday row to the Issues table, records a predicted click-to-open band before the send, and publishes a report page through the Publish Queue. Todoist gets a task with the link, the same hand-off you use now.

Part 4 · What I found

Weak spots in your data today

I looked at what the pack would pull this week. Two things need handling before Monday can be trusted.

Live Music is full of repeats and cancelled shows

Of the first 40 rows for the next four days, 12 were marked Likely Cancelled and at least 6 more were exact repeats of another row. Rosy Bones, Mel Washington, Derek Cribb, Jeff Bateman and Pterocat each appear twice, and The Almost Every Wednesday Band three times.

The Wednesday cleanup you already run is what keeps Thursday's block honest. Monday's Sunday run would be working with whatever that cleanup left behind, so the pack has to remove cancelled shows and repeats itself. You can see the effect in the sample: Wednesday Oct 14 ended up with a single clean show, because the others were cancelled or conflicting rows.

No Dinner Club date after September 23

The Dinner Club Dates table has nothing newer. The Monday block would show nothing until a date is entered. That's one more weekly thing to keep up, or Monday drops the block.

Monday and Thursday could drift into each other

The weekend overlaps both. My rule: Monday covers Monday to Thursday in full, Thursday covers Friday to Sunday in full. Monday only teases two or three weekend items.

Your picks are the whole engine

If you skip a week, there is no ranked list and no lead. The pack won't make one for you. The fallback is a short week: Week Ahead, Music and the poll, with a note that the ranking returns next Monday. The rule from the plan still applies: a missed Monday should cost nothing.
