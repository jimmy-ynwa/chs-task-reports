Seven this week. Three came off liquor-license filings, and one of those is a Mount Pleasant Italian spot moving because its landlord more than tripled the rent. And The Mason Jar closes Sunday.

Rumored — members only, not confirmed yet

  • Cuoco Pazzo Trattoria · Mount Pleasant — Cuoco Pazzo's finally got a landlord problem behind it — the Mount Pleasant Italian spot is relocating to the old Eleven81 space on Oakland Market Road after its previous landlord more than tripled the rent (Post and Courier).
  • Jack's Diner · Downtown — The long-closed Jack's Cafe near the College of Charleston looks like it's getting a second act — the Legend Deli team is said to be reviving it as Jack's Diner, a nod to original owner Jack Sewell, who ran the place for decades (Post and Courier, WhatNow).
  • Locals Sushi & Sports Pub · Johns Island — Locals Sushi & Sports Pub is opening its fourth Charleston-area location on Johns Island, taking over the old Blu Smoke Pit BBQ spot in Live Oak Square (Post and Courier, WhatNow).

  • solidcore · Mount Pleasant — National fitness chain (sold for ~$90-100M in 2023) making its Charleston debut, banner now up in Mount Pleasant.
  • South Island Pizza · Johns Island — 1,398 SF, signed lease in Johns Island's South Island Market

Now open

  • Revolving Sushi Kingdom · Mount Pleasant — 100+-dish conveyor-belt sushi bar where food runners arrive on animal-shaped trains

Closing

  • The Mason Jar by Fatboys · West Ashley — Twelve years of Southern comfort on Ashley River Road, closing Sept. 27
