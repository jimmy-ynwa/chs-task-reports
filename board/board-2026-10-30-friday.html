No new openings with a real story landed this week, so this one goes to the backlog: three moves still at the rumor stage, three openings getting closer, and one downtown bar that has cut back to four days a week.

Rumored, members only

  • Chef Loong Dim Sum (name unconfirmed) · Mount Pleasant — West Ashley's dim sum house is taking the old White Duck Taco building on Johnnie Dodds for a second room.
  • Cuoco Pazzo Trattoria · Mount Pleasant — Cuoco Pazzo's finally got a landlord problem behind it — the Mount Pleasant Italian spot is relocating to the old Eleven81 space on Oakland Market Road after its previous landlord more than tripled the rent (Post and Courier).
  • Jack's Diner · Downtown — The long-closed Jack's Cafe near the College of Charleston looks like it's getting a second act — the Legend Deli team is said to be reviving it as Jack's Diner, a nod to original owner Jack Sewell, who ran the place for decades (Post and Courier, WhatNow).

Still coming

  • Angel's & Emily · West Ashley (Avondale) — One roof, two restaurants — biscuits by day, NYC's Pizza Loves Emily by night, in the old Ruby Sunshine spot
  • CurrentBurger · Downtown — Smashburgers and hand-spun shakes steps from the Waterfront Park fountain
  • Disco Dragon Books · Mount Pleasant — The eighth Gather MTP tenant announced, not the first — and the only bookstore among them

Closing watch

  • Big John's Tavern (rumored, not confirmed) · Downtown — Cut from seven days a week to four, with three lawsuits pending and a $250,000 founding investor suing his own partners

No confirmed closings this week. Big John's hasn't said it's closing; the numbers above are why it's on the list.
