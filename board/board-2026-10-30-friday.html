Step 1 — the title
Theatre 99 is back, in Mount Pleasant
Step 2 — the body

Seven this week. Theatre 99 is doing improv in Mount Pleasant now, Quarter French finally has an opening date, and Charleston Place is closing two of its restaurants.

Opening

  • Quarter French · Downtown — Opens Thursday, Nov. 5 at 40 Broad, next door to Blind Tiger (same owners). An all-day French café and bistro with a greenhouse-style courtyard under a retractable roof. Thursday–Sunday, 8 a.m.–11 p.m. to start, daily in the new year.
  • Theatre 99 · Mount Pleasant — Back as of Oct. 28 at The Venue MTP, 627 Johnnie Dodds. The Meeting Street building sold after 21 years, so the improv moved across the bridge. Parking is free.

Now open

  • Cheeky · Downtown — Neighborhood Dining Group's seafood restaurant (the Husk and Delaney Oyster House team) opened Sept. 30 at 151 Meeting. Lunch and dinner Wednesday–Sunday for now, seven days expected by mid-November.
  • Loong Yan · Mount Pleasant — Chef Loong's second restaurant is in its soft opening in the old White Duck Taco building on Johnnie Dodds. 150-plus seats and a window where you can watch the dumplings get made.
  • Angel's & Emily · West Ashley (Avondale) — Biscuits by day, Detroit-style pizza by night, at 835 Savannah Hwy. On weekends you can order off both menus at the same table.
  • Cuoco Pazzo · Mount Pleasant — Reopened at 1181 Oakland Market Road after the old landlord more than tripled the rent.

Closing

  • Charleston Place · Downtown — The Palmetto Cafe and the Thoroughbred Club close at the end of 2026. A new restaurant, a pub and a lobby bar are planned for 2028.

Seen something open or close that we missed? Tell us in the comments.
