Quarter French opens, Dillard's packs up
Step 2 — the body

Seven this week. Three places opened their doors in the last two days, two more have dates, and two longtime names are on the way out.

Now open

  • Quarter French, French Quarter — Opened Nov. 5 at 40 Broad St, next door to sister bar Blind Tiger. The draw is the courtyard: a greenhouse-style room with the original brick walls and a retractable roof. Thursday to Sunday, 8 AM to 11 PM for now, daily starting in the new year.
  • Char House Social, James Island — A Texas-style steakhouse from the Harlow team, open as of today at 1234 Camp Rd in the old Smoky Oak space. Dinner Monday through Saturday, open till midnight Friday and Saturday.
  • Jack's Diner, Downtown — The Legend Deli crew brought the old Jack's Cafe on George St back as an all-day diner. Open every day, 7:30 AM to 9 PM, breakfast all day.

Opening

  • Alteño, Downtown — Chef Johnny Curiel's Mexican restaurant (the Denver original is Michelin-recommended) opens Nov. 12 at 89 Cannon St, inside The Charlee. Daily 3 to 10 PM, with small bites and drink specials early on weekdays.
  • Chuy's, North Charleston — The Tex-Mex chain opens Nov. 18 at 7811 Rivers Ave, in the building that was South Carolina's last Bahama Breeze.

Closing

  • Dillard's, West Ashley — Closing signs went up this week at the Citadel Mall store, Charleston's last Dillard's after about 34 years. No final day yet. The store is pointing shoppers to the Northwoods outlet.
  • Palmetto Cafe and the Thoroughbred Club, Downtown — Both close at the end of the year as The Charleston Place finishes its top-to-bottom renovation. Three new spots arrive in 2028: a signature restaurant, a pub and a new lobby bar. The Perch rooftop bar stays open in the meantime.

Seen something open or close that we missed? Tell us in the comments.
