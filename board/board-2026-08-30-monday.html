What was filtered

  • Quality Score 4 and up only. Anything at 3 or below never made the query.
  • Skip, Duplicate and Past excluded on Status. Likely Cancelled excluded on Listing Status.
  • Live Music category excluded — that's Wednesday's post, and the Live Music table is authoritative for shows.
  • Eighteen Children's Museum drop-in sessions collapsed into one line. They're legitimately 4-star but they're daily museum programming, not events, and listing them individually would have doubled the post.

Duplicate rows worth knowing about

The Airtable has real duplicates, and this post had to dedupe by hand. Mount Pleasant Farmers Market Sept 1 appears twice (different Venue spellings). Sweet Jessamine Flower Truck Sept 5 appears twice. Pantheon Sept 5 appears twice with slightly different titles. In the on-sale window it's worse: Bayside, Sugarland, Saxsquatch, Lily Rose and Ashley McBryde each appear two or three times, and The Fab Four appears three times.

The members' calendar build has billing-normalization logic that catches exactly this. Airtable does not. Either that logic moves upstream into the scrape, or every downstream reader repeats the work.
