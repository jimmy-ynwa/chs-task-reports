The post

Three filings, three closings, one brewery

A liquor licence sweep dropped thirteen new filings into the tracker Wednesday. Most are LLCs with no story attached yet. Three of them are stories.

And the closings column finally has weight. None of it is good news.

New this week — rumored

Nothing below is announced. A filing is evidence of intent, not a statement of it. Members only — don't repeat these as fact.

  • The Copper Table — Downtown. Full liquor going into the Maritime Building's ground floor on East Bay, the restaurant space that's sat empty since IHOP left in 2020. (Name comes off the filing entity, not a sign out front.)
  • The old Bohemian Bull — Mount Pleasant. Bohemian Bull has been sold, and the restaurant says the new owners have different plans. The buyer's full-liquor filing landed ten days after the LLC was formed. (Trade name unresolved.)
  • Chef Loong Dim Sum — Mount Pleasant. West Ashley's dim sum house is taking the old White Duck Taco building on Johnnie Dodds for a second room.

Closing

  • Big John's Tavern — Downtown. Cut from seven days a week to four, with three lawsuits pending and a $250,000 founding investor suing his own partners. (Rumored. Nobody has said the word closed.)
  • Theatre 99 — Downtown. Final show Sept. 12 after 21 years, with no new home announced yet.
  • The Mason Jar by Fatboys — West Ashley. Twelve years of Southern comfort on Ashley River Road, closing Sept. 27.

Still coming

  • Two Blokes Brewing — Cannonborough-Elliotborough. A small-batch experimental system downtown while the core beers stay in Mount Pleasant.

Now open

Nothing flipped open this week.
