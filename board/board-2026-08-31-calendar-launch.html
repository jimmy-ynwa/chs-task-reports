The post

The calendar just doubled

Sixty days instead of thirty. Which sounds like a small change until you look at what's sitting in the back half of it.

Sugarland at the Coliseum. Turnpike Troubadours at Firefly. Trampled by Turtles at The Refinery. The Red Clay Strays at Credit One with The Revivalists. All October. All on sale right now.

That's the entire point of the second month. The near weeks were never the problem — you already know what you're doing Saturday. What you miss is the show that quietly went on sale in August for a night in October, and by the time it crosses your feed the good seats are gone and you're looking at the upper bowl.

So everything past day 30 is badged On sale now, with a filter that shows you only those. There are 157 of them today, out of 1,130 listings total.

Fair warning on the far end: it's mostly ticketed rooms out there — the Music Hall, the Gaillard, Credit One, the PAC — because those places sell seats and publish early. Your neighborhood bar books three weeks ahead, so that layer fills in as venues announce it. Nothing's missing. It just hasn't happened yet.
