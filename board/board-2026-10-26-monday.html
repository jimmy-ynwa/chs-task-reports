Halloween lands on a Saturday this year, so the costume parties start Wednesday and keep going through Sunday. About 200 things are on the calendar this week. Here's what's worth your night.

The one to plan around is Charleston's Grand Gullah-Geechee Hoodoo Ball, back for its sequel Saturday at Our Place On 17 in Mount Pleasant. For a quieter evening, Sunset on the Stono at the Dill Sanctuary on Thursday. And clocks fall back an hour early Sunday morning, so the long night gets an extra hour of sleep.
