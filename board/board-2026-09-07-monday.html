The week, all at once

Ninety-odd things on the calendar between now and Sunday, and the two that matter most are both on a clock.

The RiverDogs are in the playoffs. Carolina League Division Series against Hickory, home at The Joe Thursday and Friday, 7:05 both nights. Win and it keeps going. Lose and the season is over and you spent it somewhere else. That's the whole pitch.

Then Saturday tries to be four days at once. Two Oktoberfests within a few miles of each other — Park Circle Oktoberfest at Holy City and BLŌKTOBERFEST at Two Blokes — plus Citadel–Charleston Southern at Johnson Hagood, Drifter Fest at The Refinery, and an R&B cooler festival on the Ashley. Pick a lane early, because you are not doing all of it.

Friday is the 25th anniversary of 9/11. Patriots Point holds its ceremony at 10 AM, and the Gaillard Center runs a volunteer meal pack that morning.
