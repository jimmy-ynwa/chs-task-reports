Step 2 — the body

Three places opened in the last week, two more have dates, and two shops with 65-plus years between them are closing. Here's the week.

Opening

  • Off Track Ice Cream — Park Circle. The Beaufain Street scoop shop's second location is going into 1054 E. Montague Ave, the old Pink Crocodile gift shop. Aiming for the end of December.
  • Tiger Lily — Mount Pleasant. The 30-year-old florist's third and biggest shop opens in January at Bowman Place on Bowman Road. Downtown and James Island stay open.

Now open

  • Portside Galley — Downtown. Build-your-own breakfast and lunch burritos at 655 Meeting St, plus coffee, parfaits and pastries. Counter only, no seats. Opened this morning.
  • Reenie's — North Charleston. Cane Pazzo chef Mark Bolchoz's new place at Blackwells Corner on Remount Road, with wood-fired cooking and a biergarten patio. Opened Nov. 20. Tuesday through Sunday, 5 to 10 PM.
  • Hachiya Ramen — Clements Ferry. The ramen shop's second location, at 654 Hopewell Dr. Opened Nov. 23, three weeks after its first planned date.

Closing

  • Mike's Bikes — James Island. Nearly 40 years at 808 Folly Road. Closing at the end of January and selling off inventory until then.
  • Charleston Lighting and Interiors — West Ashley. The locally owned lighting showroom at 1640 Sam Rittenberg Blvd is going out of business after 26 years. The liquidation sale is on, with discounts at 50% as of last week.

Seen something open or close that we missed? Tell us in the comments.
