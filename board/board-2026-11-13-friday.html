Step 1 — the title
Alteño opens, Ruby Tuesday's gone
Step 2 — the body

Six this week, and nearly all of it already happened: five places opened or reopened since late October, and West Ashley lost Charleston's last Ruby Tuesday.

Now open

  • Alteño, Cannon Street — Chef Johnny Curiel's Mexican restaurant opened Thursday at 89 Cannon St, inside The Charlee. The Denver original is Michelin-recommended. Reservations are open.
  • Palmetto Moon, King Street — The Charleston brand that started as a Citadel Mall kiosk in 2002 opened its first downtown store at 360 King St on Saturday, Nov. 7. It's the fifth Palmetto Moon in the Charleston area.
  • Locals Sushi & Sports Pub, Johns Island — The fourth Locals soft-opened Wednesday at Live Oak Square, 1804 Crowne Commons Way, in the old Blu Smoke Pit BBQ space.
  • Tru Blue Wings, Mount Pleasant — Back open on Johnnie Dodds as of Nov. 6 after almost four months of renovations: a redone bar, a new patio, and 10 draft taps instead of 3. Seafood Friday returns today.
  • Harlow & Pearl, Clements Ferry — The coffee shop's third location opened Oct. 28 in the Clements Ferry area.

Closing

  • Ruby Tuesday, West Ashley — The location at 1953 Savannah Hwy has closed for good. It was the last Ruby Tuesday in Charleston.
