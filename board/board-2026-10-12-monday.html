Saturday is the day to plan around. The Stingrays open their home season at the North Charleston Coliseum against the Greenville Swamp Rabbits (first 2,500 fans get a magnet schedule), and the 17th annual Charleston Pride Parade comes down King Street that morning.

Pride runs all week, too: Pitch a Friend at Share House on Tuesday, Pride Night at Southern Screams on Wednesday, a kickoff at Taco Boy on Friday, then a pool party at the Starlight Motor Inn and the Divas on Tap drag brunch at Edmund's Oast on Sunday.
