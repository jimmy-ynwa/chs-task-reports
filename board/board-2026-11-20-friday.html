An Irish tavern opens tonight, Mike's Bikes is closing
Step 2 — the body

Four openings and three closings this week, and one of the closings has been fixing Charleston's bikes since 1987.

Opening

  • Reenie's, North Charleston — An Irish-coastal tavern from Mark and Ariana Bolchoz of Cane Pazzo, in the old Dashi space at 1262 Remount Rd. It opens tonight: wood-fired hearth, Guinness on tap, plenty of tables held for walk-ins. Tuesday through Sunday, 5 to 10 PM.

Now open

  • Chuy's, North Charleston — The Tex-Mex chain opened Wednesday at 7811 Rivers Ave, near Northwoods Mall. It took over South Carolina's last Bahama Breeze, which closed Oct. 26.
  • Fête, Downtown — A wine garden in the courtyard next to Kudu at 4 Vanderhorst St, from the Kudu and Lodi Coffee owners and Wine & Company's Joshua Walker. Wine, local beer and charcuterie sandwiches, 4 to 10 PM, closed Tuesdays.
  • Philosophia, Point Hope — The Mount Pleasant Greek restaurant's second location is open at 846 Gates Crossing Way, off Clements Ferry Rd. Same menu, lunch and dinner Tuesday through Saturday.

Closing

  • Mike's Bikes, James Island — Closing at the end of January. Mike Keck opened on Hayne St. in 1987 and has been at 808 Folly Rd for about 25 years. He says he's near retirement, the lease is up and retail costs keep climbing. All stock is being sold off.
  • Ruby Tuesday, West Ashley — Charleston's last Ruby Tuesday, at 1953 Savannah Hwy, has closed for good. The sign on the door sends diners to Walterboro or Orangeburg.
  • Dillard's, Citadel Mall — Closing signs are up at the store after 34 years, as MUSC turns the mall into a medical campus. No closing date yet. The Northwoods Mall store in North Charleston stays open.

Seen something open or close that we missed? Tell us in the comments.
