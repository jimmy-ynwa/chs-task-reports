01The short version

Nothing improved

Four new listings came into the neighborhood, the home closest to this one cut again, and not one attached home here has closed in six weeks. The value we found on the first report still holds. The competition around it got heavier, and that's what moves the launch number.

02The diff

Five things that changed

  1. The closest rival cut again, to $359,900Down 16.1% from its $429,000 start, 216 days on market. It's eighty square feet larger, in the same subdivision. Every buyer who sees this home will see that one first.
  2. Four new listingsTwo of the four are direct three-bedroom competition.
  3. Nothing has closed in six weeksEight homes competing and no completed sale is the clearest measure of how slow this pocket has become.
  4. Both under-contract homes are still pendingIf either falls through, it comes back as competition priced just under this one.
  5. The home is vacant and we've walked itCondition report below.

The cuts are getting deeper

Three weeks ago, listings in the broader lane that had cut were down a median of $24,950. Here, today, the four sellers who cut are down a median of $40,550, about 10%. This market isn't asking sellers to be patient. It's charging them for it.

04The walk-through

What we found, and what it costs

Nothing structural, nothing mechanical, nothing that would scare an inspector. A short cosmetic list.

  • A thorough professional clean. First in line. Three years of tenants leaves a film that photographs badly, and an empty house hides nothing.
  • Touch-up paint throughout. Empty walls magnify every scuff.
  • A short list of minor repairs. Better handled now than written into a buyer's repair request.
  • Photos and floor plans only after all three are done. The photos are the whole first showing for most buyers, and they only get made once.
What it means for price

Cosmetic work doesn't lift this home into fully-renovated territory. It keeps it out of the 298-day outcome, where a tired presentation and an ambitious price ended at $345,000.

05The number

$369,000If speed matters more

The value didn't change. The competition did. With the closest rival at $359,900 for eighty more square feet and two more three-bedrooms on the board, we trimmed $5,000 off the launch. It puts the home under the $375,000 mark buyers actually filter on.

Why we're not testing a higher number

40% of comparable sales went under contract in the first thirty days, and the sellers who dragged past four months almost all started above the market. In this pocket, the first thirty days are the whole opportunity.
