A neighborhood guide nobody else had

The Charleston Insider's Guide covers every major part of the area: what a home costs, what it feels like, flood risk, the commute, and who it's right for. Market numbers are built from MLS data and refreshed on a schedule.

It did two jobs. It answered the questions people ask before they ever call an agent, and it gave them a reason to hand over an email address.
