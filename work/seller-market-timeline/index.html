01Why this lane

An earlier version measured the wrong homes

The first pass looked at a larger, pricier group, with a median list price near $540,000. Those homes aren't what this buyer is choosing between. This version narrows to the real competitive set. The picture is a bit slower and more honest, and every number now matches the pricing report.

02Pricing power

Right-priced homes still sell close to asking

98.9%Of final list price, median
97.2%Of original list price, median

The gap between those two numbers is the whole story. Sellers who set the right number on day one gave up almost nothing. Sellers who didn't gave up a price cut first, then negotiated from there.

What moves the needle

28 of the 52 sellers, 54%, had to cut before they found a buyer. The median cut was $19,550, about 4%.

67% went under contract within 60 days. The ones that took four months or more are overwhelmingly the listings that started above the market.

One number worth knowing

Several sellers withdrew and relisted, which resets the public clock but not what an agent sees in the full history. Measured cumulatively, the median stretches from 44.5 days to 52, and the share carrying 120+ days jumps from 13.5% to 21%. Buyers' agents see the cumulative number.

05What it means

A realistic marketing timeline

  1. Weeks 1–4 on marketShowings are front-loaded. 40.4% of comparable sales went under contract in this window, the largest single group.
  2. About six weeks to contract at the median paceThe honest middle of 52 real outcomes. In this subdivision, the two homes priced right this summer went under contract in 33 and 21 days.
  3. Expect to net close to asking98.9% of list. The 97.2% of original list is what happens to sellers who start too high.
  4. 30–45 days from contract to closingAppraisal, inspection and underwriting on a financed purchase. Cash buyers move faster.
  5. About 2.5 to 3.5 months, list to closeIf priced to the market. Pricing above the comps stretches every step, and more than half the time it costs a cut anyway.
