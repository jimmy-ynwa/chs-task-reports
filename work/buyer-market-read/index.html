01Start here

The two communities with real inventory aren't the one we started with

The older community next door, Kiawah River Estates, has barely moved, and that's good news: every home we flagged in August is still available and two came down. Dunes West gets a full workup below. The first choice, Kiawah River, got tighter.

One piece of MLS vocabulary matters

"Contingent" means an accepted offer with a condition still to clear. Those homes are spoken for, so we count them as gone. Counted honestly, 8 of the 9 Kiawah River homes in range are already under contract. The only one available today is a 509-square-foot cottage.

02The builder floor

A moving target that keeps coming back into range

New homes here sell straight from the builder and close the day they hit the MLS, so they're a clean read on the true entry price.

February to August the builder's price climbed $178,527. Then a smaller home came in at $1,525,000 and went under contract the same week. The budget isn't the obstacle. Readiness is.

What we'd put in place if this community stays on the list

  • A line into the builder's sales team for releases before they reach the MLS.
  • A lender letter in hand, so an offer can go the same day.
  • A short, honest list of what you'd say yes to, agreed before a clock is running.

05The island question

Infrastructure, now with a date

They asked whether anything real was coming closer than the mainland. No big-box retail, and we don't see that changing. What is changing is medical and everyday services: the island's first emergency room opens this month, with a 50-bed hospital planned on the same site, a new elementary school under construction, and a funded road connector finished.

The honest summary

If the test is emergency care, groceries, dinner and a hardware run without leaving the island, it's trending toward yes. If the test is a Costco run without a bridge, it stays no.
