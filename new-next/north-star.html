03

Selling the unsellable

Nobody buys "relationship building." It's unsellable as a noun, and everyone claims it. You don't sell the soft skill — you sell the system the soft skill got encoded into.

The BD instinct isn't the product. It's the thing that let you build a better product than a technical person would have. The sponsor pre-call sequence, the favor-to-sponsor pivot, the "no personal email until they click or reply" gate — the relationship wisdom is load-bearing in the design decisions. Teach the system; the judgment rides inside it. Show the how, and the why quietly proves the career.

05

The weekly rhythm

Three posts a week, Tuesday through Thursday, never daily. Daily is the guru hamster wheel the platform stopped rewarding and your temperament won't sustain. Three save-worthy posts beat seven reflexive ones. Tuesday to Thursday is the engagement sweet spot and weekends are the weakest days for a professional audience, so the old Saturday slot is retired. Wednesday is the single strongest day, so it carries the flagship. Post mid-morning, around 10 to 11 ET, and hold the window.

07

Standing guardrails

→

LinkedIn is discovery, never the destination. If it throttles you tomorrow, you shrug — the relationship was never banked there.

→

The profile is part of every post. Headline says what you build. It files you into the right room before a word of the post is read.

→

Reject the vulnerability loop. No performative emotional striptease. Expertise travels now — you don't have to bleed for reach.

→

Sustainability is a strategy input. A cadence you'll ghost in two weeks is worse than a lighter one you'll keep. Design for the you that gets bored.

→

Charleston stays off this feed. CHS, TFG, and the local layer are a different audience and a different job. This surface is New NEXT only.

→

Original and on-lane, or don't post. The algorithm rewards what AI can't fake: your real screenshots, your own takes. And on-topic 8 of 10 beats 6 of 10 by 30%, so drift isn't neutral, it taxes you. The firewall above is now a ranking advantage, not just discipline.
