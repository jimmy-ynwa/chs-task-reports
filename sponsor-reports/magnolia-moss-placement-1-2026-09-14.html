What we think drove it

This is a first placement doing what first placements rarely do.

  • The fit. This audience skews female, older, and affluent — exactly the people planning the events you rent for. That's a shorter path from reader to customer than most advertisers get.
  • The slot. Your logo at the top of the email, your block seated ahead of the lead story — that position consistently outperforms placements further down the issue.
  • The copy. Naming table numbers and tumblers instead of "custom sourcing" gave readers something concrete to picture wanting. Your correction on that line made the ad better — worth carrying into the next two.

One honest caveat: the issue landed Friday instead of Thursday and had its best overall click rate in three weeks, so some of the lift is the whole issue performing well, not just your ad. Even stripping that out, your share of everyone who clicked anything still comes out to 7%, so the underlying result holds.
