Wire the New NEXT list

The capture page is live and the LinkedIn profile points at it, but the form has nowhere to deliver. This closes that loop, so the 30-day posting plan launches into a working funnel instead of a dead end.

One sitting · ~45-60 minutes

Before you start

You'll need: access to jimmyfarrell.com in Cloudflare, the page's source folder (if you no longer have it, say so and I'll rebuild the page), and about an hour. Email routing on jimmy@jimmyfarrell.com is already live, which is what unblocks step 1.

Phase 1 — The account

A separate Kit account, isolated from CHS, so nothing entangles when CHS sunsets.

  1. Confirm the address actually receives
    Send a test email to jimmy@jimmyfarrell.com from any account and confirm it lands in your CHS inbox. Do this first. If routing is silently broken, every step after this fails in a confusing way.
  2. Open a new Kit account with that address
    Sign up fresh at kit.com using jimmy@jimmyfarrell.com. Do not use your CHS address, Kit ties one account to one email and you want these fully separate. Choose the free Newsletter plan.
  3. Set the sender identity to you, not the entity
    Account name and from-name: Jimmy Farrell. This matches the posting-identity decision, you're the brand, Farrell Digital Ventures is the backdrop.

Phase 2 — The form

One form, double opt-in, with the confirmation email doing the welcome work the free plan won't automate.

  1. Create an inline form
    Name it New NEXT List.
    Grow → Landing Pages & Forms → Create → Form → Inline
  2. Keep double opt-in ON
    Leave Send confirmation email checked and Auto-confirm new subscribers unchecked. You're pointing a cold audience at a brand-new sending domain, and double opt-in protects deliverability from day one.
  3. Make the confirmation email carry the welcome
    This is the free-plan workaround. Sequences need a paid tier, but the confirmation email is a form setting, not an automation. Rewrite it so it does real work: who you are, what they'll get, how often. It's the first thing every subscriber reads.
    Free-plan note: also expect "Powered by Kit" branding and no Zapier integration until you upgrade to Creator (~$33/mo). Neither blocks you at zero subscribers.
  4. Grab the numeric form ID
    Open the form and read the number in the browser URL. That's the ID the page needs.
    The gotcha that already burned us once: use the numeric ID, not the alphanumeric UID from the embed snippet. The alphanumeric one produces a form that submits and silently fails, no error, no subscriber.

Phase 3 — Wire and deploy

Connect the live page to the live form, then redeploy.

  1. Paste the ID into the page
    In index.html, find the commented KIT FORM INTEGRATION POINT block and replace REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_FORM_ID in the form action with your numeric ID. Leave the rest of the markup alone, the styling already wraps Kit's fields.
  2. Redeploy to Cloudflare Pages
    Zip the folder and drag-drop as a wipe-replace, your standard deploy pattern. The zip doubles as the recoverable master.
  3. Test it end to end with a real address
    Submit the form from the live site. Confirm three things: the confirmation email arrives, clicking it confirms you, and the subscriber shows up in Kit. If any link in that chain breaks, it's almost always the form ID.

Phase 4 — Sending domain

Not needed to capture subscribers. Needed before you send your first broadcast.

  1. Add jimmyfarrell.com as the sending domain in Kit
    Kit will hand you CNAME records to add.
    Settings → Email → Sending domain
  2. Add the CNAMEs in Cloudflare
    Add them to the jimmyfarrell.com zone. This is a different zone from chshappenings.com, so your standing "never touch the CHS DNS" rule isn't in play here. But jimmyfarrell.com now has MX records from Email Routing, so add only what Kit asks for and don't disturb the MX entries.
    Hard rule: if Kit asks you to set DMARC to p=none, don't. Keep the stronger p=quarantine. Kit requests the weaker policy for convenience, it isn't a requirement.
  3. Set the CAN-SPAM footer address
    Every broadcast needs a physical mailing address. Use a virtual mailbox (iPostal1 was the plan) rather than your home address.

Done when

  • A real email submitted at jimmyfarrell.com produces a confirmation email and a confirmed subscriber in Kit
  • The confirmation email reads like a welcome, not a system notice
  • The LinkedIn Website field still points at jimmyfarrell.com with the link text "See how it's built"
  • You know your subscriber count, which is your honest baseline of zero before the 30-day plan starts moving it

Why this is the blocker

The whole strategy is "LinkedIn is discovery, the list is the business." Right now the discovery half works and the business half doesn't exist. Launching the posting cadence before this is wired would spend a first month of fresh attention on a form that goes nowhere, and a launch month is the one thing you can't rerun.
