Caveats
  • A filing is an intention, not a date — it says nothing about when (or whether) a business opens.
  • South Carolina doesn't require re-advertising for a renewal at the same business/location — so an already-open business filing here means something changed (new class, ownership transfer, or premises change), not that it's a new business.
  • This sweep can't see any business that doesn't serve alcohol — it's not a complete view of openings.
