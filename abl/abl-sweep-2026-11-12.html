Caveats:

A filing is an intention to apply, not an opening date — and SC doesn't require re-advertising to renew the same business at the same location, so an already-open business showing up here means something changed (new spot, upgraded licence, ownership transfer, or a premises change), not that it's brand new.

This sweep can only see businesses applying for an alcohol licence — it's blind to anything that doesn't serve alcohol.
