A filing is an intention to apply, not an opening date — and nothing here says whether it's a new application, a renewal, or a transfer.

South Carolina doesn't require re-advertising to renew a licence at the same business and address, so a filing from an already-open business signals a change (new owner, added licence class, altered premises) — not noise.

This sweep can't see any business that doesn't serve alcohol, so it's not a complete picture of what's opening in Charleston.
