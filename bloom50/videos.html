The 73 clips, before anybody sees them

Kim — these are the demonstration videos that would sit next to each exercise in the app. I picked them by reading titles and channels, not by watching all 73. For women over fifty that isn't good enough on its own. That's what this page is for.

How to do this. Go down the page and watch anything that looks off. Tap a thumbnail and it opens on YouTube. About forty minutes — and you don't have to do it in one go.

Two buttons on every clip. Fine if it's good, Flag if it's wrong, too aggressive, or just not how you teach it. The bar at the top keeps count, and Not looked at yet shows you only what's left.

It remembers. Close the page, come back tomorrow, and you'll pick up exactly where you stopped — on this device, in this browser.

When you're done, press Send my list to Jimmy and it opens an email with the flagged ones already written out.

Start with the 11 marked Closest match. Those are the ones where your name for a move isn't what the rest of the world calls it, so I had to settle for the nearest ordinary version.
