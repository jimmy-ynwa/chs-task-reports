Part two

Building the app, step by step

Seven phases. Two of them block everything else and both are account setup, so they come first. Video curation runs in parallel from day one because it's the longest single stretch of work in the project and nothing else waits on it.

The north star, so scope doesn't creep

It's a training ledger: Capture, Reflect, Guide. Log the workout fast, see whether it's working, know what to do next. If a proposed feature isn't one of those three, it's bloat and it doesn't ship in v1.

Already out of scope and staying out: running, Strava, nutrition tracking, per-client program assignment, messaging, social feeds, badges, push notifications.
