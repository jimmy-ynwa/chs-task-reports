What this inventory is telling us

Everything on this page is on one level. Nothing else made the page.

Something new: 1116 Bexley Street came on the market yesterday at $675,000 — single story, 4 bed, 3 bath, 1,825 sq ft, with a detached guest cottage and the furniture included. It is the first genuinely new one-level home this watch has surfaced since it started tracking, and it is the only home on the board that has not cut its price. It has been listed for one day.

The rest of the board has been sitting. Four of the five homes here have reduced from their original ask, $201,000 in cuts between them, and the median is 79 days on market. 5112 Princeton has been out 126 days and taken $50,000 off. 4615 O'Hear has been out 61 days and taken $61,000 off. Nobody in this size and price band is holding firm.

Which is what makes Bexley's ask interesting. At $370 per square foot it is the second-priciest home on the page per foot, and it is asking that on day one. The only home above it, 4615 O'Hear at $390, is a smaller floor plan that has already given back eight percent. The board's median is $346. Bexley is priced like the market is tighter than the last four months say it is.

Two of the five now come with a second dwelling — Bexley's guest cottage and 4953 Baylor's permitted ADU. Neither is required, but that is a real shift in what one-level inventory looks like around the circle.

4512 Overbrook remains the only one under $600,000 and remains under contract. One note on photos: 4615 O'Hear's yard images include two flagged AI-generated renderings, and 4512 Overbrook has several virtually staged rooms.
