The honest read

A strong start, a dip I own, and a comeback.

What worked

The launch and the finish. Sept. 11 drew 54 email clicks with named pieces and the hosting payoff. Oct. 29 came back with your photo, three named dessert pieces and a Thanksgiving reason to act now.

Your audience is here. When the ad shows a specific piece and a reason to want it now, readers click.

What didn't

The middle sagged, and that's on me. Sept. 24 ran without its photo. Oct. 8 had no headline, a December ask in early October and a homepage link. Clicks fell to 7.

The fix was the ad, not your business: the rebuilt Oct. 29 ad drew three times the share of clickers.

What I'd change next time

  • Lead with one named piece and your photo. Every ad that did that worked.
  • Ask three to four weeks ahead of the date that matters. Thanksgiving, holiday parties, spring weddings.
  • Link straight to the collection, never the home page.
