The honest read

Clicks fell, and by a lot. Your first ad has settled at 73 clicks. This one drew 23. I'd rather you hear that from me than work it out yourself.

It wasn't a quiet week. This issue actually clicked a little better overall than the September 11 issue did. Readers were clicking; fewer of them clicked this ad. So the difference is in the ad and where it sat, not the audience.

Three things changed from the first one. First, position. Your first ad had top billing, with your name at the top of the email and the block above the lead story. This one ran mid-issue, which is how the Secondary slot works, and the web card sat at the bottom of the stories instead of the top. Second, the angle. The first ad named things people could picture on their own table, like table numbers and tumblers. This one sold the service: delivery, setup and breakdown. That's a real advantage, but it's harder to picture. Third, it ran as text only, with no photo of your pieces.

For perspective: a sponsored block here usually gets one or two clicks for every thousand people who open the issue. This one got 2.4, still above that range. Your first got 6.6, one of the strongest results I've measured.

What I'd change for placement 3 (October 8)

Go back to the pieces. October 8 is your Primary, so you're back at the top. Let's lead with two or three specific pieces a reader can picture, the way the first ad did.

Give it a season. Holiday party season is close. A hosting-season hook gives people a reason to click now instead of someday.

Add a photo. One good shot of your pieces set up for a party will stop the scroll better than any line I can write.
