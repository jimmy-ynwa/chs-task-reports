The honest read

What worked

You led the issue. First thing under the weather in the email, and first thing on every web story. That's 8,696 times your name was seen.

The links were tagged, so these visits show up as CHS Happenings in your own stats.

What didn't

Clicks fell again, from 19 to 6. The whole issue clicked a little less this week, and the lead story pulled almost half of everyone who clicked. But your slice of all clickers fell from 2.45% to 0.93%, so most of this is the ad, not the issue.

My read: a third run to the same readers, a December ask in early October, and a soft “start the conversation” button to the home page with no headline to stop the scroll.

What I'd change next time

  • Show one piece and name it. Your best result led with specific pieces. One great bar or dessert display, photographed and named in a headline, beats a list of twelve categories.
  • Send the button somewhere to browse. A link to your collection lets readers shop tonight. “Start the conversation” asks them to commit before they've seen anything.
  • Give a nearer reason. On Oct. 29, Friendsgiving and November gatherings are weeks away, not months. That's an easier yes than December.
