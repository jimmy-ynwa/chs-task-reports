The honest read

What worked

The voice did the work. A first-person ad that names the problem out loud, in your words, got 59 people to click from the email alone.

It sat in the top slot, with a real class photo and one clear ask: come to a free class.

What didn't

The web barely moved. 1,570 web views turned into 4 clicks. Four of the six stories with your card drew nothing.

And the email link wasn't tagged, so those 59 visits won't show up as CHS Happenings in your own site stats. That one's on me.

What I'd change next time

  • Tag every link. Oct. 15 goes out with a tracking tag on the email link, so you can see what those visitors did after they landed.
  • Send the web cards to the same page. The email pointed to your training page. The web cards pointed to your home page. One door is better than two.
  • Tell me who showed up. If new faces came to Wednesday's class from this, I'd love to know. Clicks are only half the story.
