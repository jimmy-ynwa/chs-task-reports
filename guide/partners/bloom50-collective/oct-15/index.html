The honest read

What worked

The ad held. 59 email clicks on Oct. 1, 60 on Oct. 15. Second showings usually fade. Yours didn't.

The new headline and the plain promise, free, nothing to sign up for, kept the door wide open.

What didn't

You got a smaller slice of a bigger crowd. This issue clicked harder than Oct. 1 (11.8% vs. 9.5%), and the lead story alone pulled 594 readers. Your share of everyone who clicked slipped from 7.8% to 6.2%.

The web is still quiet. 2,347 web views, 7 clicks. Four of the five stories drew nothing.

What I'd change next time

  • Give Oct. 29 a reason to come now. It's the last of the three. A date, a first-timer welcome, or the Monday class gives readers a deadline instead of someday.
  • Keep the voice. First person, from you, about the woman reading it. Two runs, same result. Don't fix what works.
  • Tell me who showed up. The links are tagged now, so your site stats will show CHS Happenings visits. New faces on a Wednesday are the real score.
