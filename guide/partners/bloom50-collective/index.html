The honest read

Strong start, strong middle, a softer finish.

What worked

All three runs beat the usual range for CHS Happenings sponsors, by a wide margin. Real class photos, a real member's words and a specific class to show up to did that.

The Oct. 15 rewrite held the line. A fresh headline kept clicks level with the launch.

What didn't

Oct. 29 slipped to 46. The whole issue clicked a bit less, and the ad repeated the same Wednesday ask a third time with no new hook. That's on me.

The web side stayed small, 14 clicks across three runs.

What I'd change next time

  • New hook every run. Repeating the same class ask wears thin by the third week.
  • Keep the faces and the quotes. They're why readers stopped scrolling.
  • Keep linking straight to the class page, tagged.
