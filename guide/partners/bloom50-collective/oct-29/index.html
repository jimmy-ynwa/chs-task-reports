The honest read

What worked

Still well above the pack. 43 people clicked from the email. A real photo of your class, a quote from a real member and a free class with a day and time attached all pulled their weight.

Every link went to your class page, tagged, so you can see these visits in your own stats.

What didn't

Clicks dipped, from 60 to 43. Part of that is the issue: everyone clicked about 13% less than on Oct. 15. Part of it is the ad. It was the third ask in a row for the same Wednesday class, with no new hook. That's on me.

The web stayed small. Three clicks from 2,196 web views. The email does the heavy lifting.

What I'd change next time

  • Give every run a new hook. A named session, a seasonal challenge or a member's story keeps a repeat reader from scrolling past.
  • Keep the real faces. Your class in the park beats any stock photo.
  • Keep the deep link. Straight to the class page, tagged, every time.
