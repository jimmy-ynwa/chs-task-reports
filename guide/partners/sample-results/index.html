The honest read

What worked

Top billing did its job. Your block sat at the top of the email and at the top of five web stories, and the people who opened the issue clicked it far above the usual rate.

The ad led with specific products instead of a general description of the business. Every link carried tracking tags, so you can see these visitors in your own analytics.

What didn't

The quieter web stories gave very little. The lead story and story 2 produced 14 of the 15 story clicks. Stories 3, 4 and 5 had 426 views between them and drew 1 click.

Clicks followed readers. Where a story pulled a crowd, your card pulled clicks.

What I'd change next time

  • Tie the ad to something specific and timely. A concrete product with a reason to act this week gives people a reason to click now.
  • Tell me what you saw on your end. I'll line your numbers up against mine so we know what those clicks turned into.
