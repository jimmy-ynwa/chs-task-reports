The honest read

Clicks dipped in the middle, then came back.

What worked

The offer. Oct. 15 gave readers something to book, 15% off with your code, and it was your best placement overall: 19 clicks.

Top billing. Sept. 17 in the Primary slot earned the biggest share of the issue's clickers, 2.6%.

What didn't

Oct. 1 asked too much, too fast. A same-night party reminder that most morning readers couldn't act on. Email clicks fell to 9.

The Secondary slot earns about half the share the Primary does with this audience.

What I'd change next time

  • Lead with something to book. A clear offer beat a celebration message. A deal, a date or a new room gives readers a reason to click today.
  • Give readers lead time. Morning readers plan their week. A few days' notice beats a same-day reminder.
  • Count what came in. Your code tells the real story. Bookings beat clicks.
