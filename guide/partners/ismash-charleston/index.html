The honest read

The email did its job. A sponsored block in this newsletter usually gets one or two clicks for every thousand people who open the issue. Yours got 1.75. That's a solid first run, in the upper half of that range. The anniversary angle helped: a real date, a real address, and a reason to show up. That gives readers something to act on.

The website was about visibility, not clicks. Your “Presented by iSmash” banner sat at the top of all five stories on the site, and 957 people saw it there. None of them clicked. That's typical for a banner at the top of a story, where readers are heading into what they came to read. On the web it works as a name and a date put in front of people, not as a link. The clicks came from the email, where people are scanning for something to do.

Keep in mind that clicks are only part of what this buys. Almost eight thousand local readers saw the name iSmash, the words “one year,” and the date October 1, whether or not they tapped anything.

What I'd suggest for placement 2

Time it to the party. The October 1 issue lands in inboxes that morning, the same day as your 5:30 celebration. A short “it's tonight” reminder is the easiest way to turn people who saw it once into people who show up.
