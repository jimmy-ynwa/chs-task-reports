The honest read

What worked

A real offer works. Email clicks went from 9 to 13 once readers had something to book: 15% off with your code.

The web finally showed up. 6 web clicks, your best of the three. The card now sits higher in each story, so part of that is the layout, not just the ad.

What didn't

The slot still matters. Your Sept. 17 Primary earned 2.6% of everyone who clicked. This Secondary got 1.3%, in a busier issue (11.8% clicked anything, vs. 6.8% then).
