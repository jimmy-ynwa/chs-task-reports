The honest read

What worked

Your name was everywhere on party day. The email plus six web stories put iSmash in front of readers 9,545 times the morning of your anniversary.

The links were tagged, so these visits show up as CHS Happenings in your own stats.

What didn't

Clicks fell from 14 to 9. This wasn't a quiet issue. It clicked better than Sept. 17. Your slice of all clickers fell by more than half.

My read: a mid-issue slot, an event most morning readers couldn't make that same night, and a second telling of the same anniversary story.

What I'd change next time

  • Give the last one a fresh reason to click. Not the anniversary again. Something new and specific to book, like a date-night deal or a group package, with a reason to do it this month.
  • Lead with the offer, not the date. “Tonight” only works for people who can go tonight. A weekend offer works for everyone who reads it.
