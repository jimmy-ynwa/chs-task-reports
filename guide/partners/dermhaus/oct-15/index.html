The honest read

What worked

The details sold it. Vendors, a grazing board, door prizes and 20% off that day gave readers a reason to put Oct. 24 on the calendar.

The lead story carried you on the web: 4 clicks from its 1,758 readers.

What didn't

Clicks undercount a free, walk-in event. RSVP is optional, so plenty of readers can just show up without ever clicking. I can't see those people. You can.

Four of the five web stories drew no clicks.

What I'd change next time

  • Check your Eventbrite RSVPs. Every link carried a CHS Happenings tag. Eventbrite will show how many RSVPs came from the newsletter.
  • Ask at the door. A quick "how'd you hear about us?" on Saturday catches the walk-ins that clicks can't.
  • Give the RSVP a reason. If there's a perk for RSVPs, like a door-prize entry, say so. It turns quiet interest into a number.
