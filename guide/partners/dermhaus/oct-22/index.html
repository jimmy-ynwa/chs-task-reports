The honest read

What worked

Top billing, right on time. Two days out, with a date, an address and 20% off, readers had a reason to act this week. Email clicks tripled, 10 to 30.

The details did the selling. Vendors, a grazing board and kids welcome made it easy to picture a Saturday morning there.

What didn't

The web card stayed quiet. Two clicks from 1,068 web views, and none from the lead ghost story. That card is mine to sharpen.

Clicks still undercount a walk-in event. Anyone who just showed up Saturday never needed to click.

What I'd change next time

  • Give the next one its own reason. With the Open Haus behind us, Nov. 5 needs a fresh hook: one named service and a reason to book before the holidays.
  • Link straight to booking. Send readers to the exact page for that service, not the home page, so the click turns into an appointment.
  • Show the place. A real photo of the office or the team beats a flyer. It makes a new practice feel familiar.
