The honest read

What worked

A deadline every reader already knows. Deductibles reset Jan. 1. Nobody had to take my word for the urgency, and 17 people went from the email to your booking page.

Your plan list answered the first question. Naming BlueCross, Aetna, Cigna, Humana, Medicare and TRICARE told readers it would likely work for them before they clicked. Your photo said "skin check" at a glance.

What didn't

Clicks slipped from 27 to 21. Fewer people clicked anything in this issue than on Nov. 5, and your ad held most of its share of them, so this was mostly a quieter week, not the ad wearing thin.

Where it lost ground: the booking page opens to a menu of visit types, so readers had to find Skin Check on their own. And 1,046 web views brought only 4 clicks.

What I'd change next time

  • Dec. 10: three weeks left, said plainly. Same deadline, now closer. "Three weeks until your deductible resets" goes in the headline.
  • Tell readers what happens after the click. One line, "Pick Skin Check, choose a time, done," removes the guesswork. If your booking system has a link that opens straight to skin checks, send it and I'll use that instead.
  • Name real openings. If you have skin check times open before Christmas, tell me the days and I'll put them in the ad. A real open slot this month is a reason to book today.
