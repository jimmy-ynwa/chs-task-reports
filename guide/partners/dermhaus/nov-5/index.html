The honest read

What worked

One clear offer. One package, a firm end date and a link straight to it. Twenty-four people clicked through from the email, for a premium gift, six weeks before Christmas.

The photo did its job. You and the team in front of the Broad Street building made the practice feel familiar before anyone read a word.

What didn't

Clicks dipped from the Open Haus, 32 to 27. The ad drew a smaller share of the issue's clickers too, so this wasn't a quiet week. The Open Haus was free and two days away. A holiday package open through Dec. 31 gave readers no reason to click this week.

Phone bookings and the Jasper market don't show up here, so clicks undercount this one.

What I'd change next time

  • Put a deadline inside the next two weeks. A book-by date before Thanksgiving week, or a set number of holiday appointments, turns someday into now.
  • Nov. 19: introduce your new aesthetician. Lead with one of the new facials, a photo of your new aesthetician at work, and a reason to book before the holidays fill up.
  • Link straight to booking that facial. Going straight to the package page worked. Keep the phone number too, and tell me if calls or the Jasper market brought anyone in.
