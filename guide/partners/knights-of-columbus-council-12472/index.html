The honest read

What worked

Clear and complete. A date, a place, a price, what it covers, and a way in for people without a foursome. That's everything a reader needs to decide.

The cause came through. Over $30,000 to local groups last year gives readers a reason beyond the golf.

What didn't

A narrow ask for a broad list. A $150 golf day speaks to a slice of readers, so clicks landed in the middle of the range, not the top.

The auction got a mention, not a button. It's the one thing every reader could join.

What I'd change next time

  • Give the auction its own link. Open to everyone, so it widens the door past golfers.
  • Run it two weeks out, then remind. More time to round up a foursome.
