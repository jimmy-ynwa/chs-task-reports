The honest read

What worked

Everything lined up. A real photo of your runners, a dated reason to show up (the Halloween Run, Oct. 28) and a link straight to the running club page.

It spoke to everyone. All faces, all paces, walkers included. A wider door than Workplace Fit, and readers walked through it: 27 email clicks, up from 8.

What didn't

The web side stayed small. Two clicks from 1,068 web views. The email does the heavy lifting for you.

The share jump is the ad, not a busier issue. The whole issue drew fewer clickers than Oct. 8.

What I'd change next time

  • Keep a date in every ad. A named run or sale with a day attached gave readers a reason to click now. The holiday run should do the same.
  • Keep the faces. Your own people in your own shirts beat a logo every time.
  • Link to the exact page. Straight to the run, the sale or the gift guide, never the home page.
