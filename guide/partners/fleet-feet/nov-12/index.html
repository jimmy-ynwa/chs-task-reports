The honest read

What worked

A question nobody could answer. “When did someone last measure your feet?” made people stop, because for most of us the answer is grade school.

Free, specific and dated. A free 30-minute fitting with a 3D scan, a reason to come before Thanksgiving, and a button straight to booking. Every click landed where people could act.

What didn't

The web card stayed quiet. One click from 1,338 web views, the same as the last few weeks. Readers act from the email, which is why the top spot paid off.

What I'd change next time

  • Keep the recipe. One clear offer, a question or a surprise up top, a date, and a link straight to where people act. That’s what moved this one.
  • Nov. 26: one Black Friday offer up top. Lead with Stuff the Buy Local Bag, put the weekend dates in the first line, and send the button straight to your Black Friday page.
  • Show it full. A photo of the bag stuffed with gear does more than a list of deals.
