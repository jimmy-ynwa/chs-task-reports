Already done · Nothing for you to decide here

Three changes I made, and why

These aren’t options. All three are already built into the ad below, and each one is based on how buyers actually think.

1

Open with a question about them, not the scanner

How buyers think: people skim past a list of features, especially one they’ve read before. A question about themselves stops them, and the answer for most people makes the case for you.

“When did someone last measure your feet?”

2

Lead with “you decide”

How buyers think: the real worry with running shoes isn’t the price. It’s paying it and still having sore feet, or getting talked into the wrong pair. Your staff already have the line that answers both, and it goes in the ad word for word.

“We give you all the tools in the toolbox, and you decide what goes home.”

3

A reason to come in now, and a real fitting in the photo

How buyers think: “someday” never comes, and a date gets people moving. Thanksgiving puts everyone on their feet, from the morning 5K to the Black Friday lines. Your running club ad also proved that your own people beat a logo. So the photo should be one real fitting, with the scanner, an Outfitter and a customer in the frame.
