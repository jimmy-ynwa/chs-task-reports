The honest read

What worked

The running club. Oct. 22 had everything: your own runners in the photo, a dated reason (the Halloween Run) and a link straight to the club page. 29 clicks, more than the first two combined.

Steady eyeballs. Your name was seen about 9,000 times every issue.

What didn't

September couldn't be counted. The button opened an email, so I could only see web taps. My setup, fixed by Oct. 8.

Workplace Fit spoke to a narrow crowd. An ask for owners and HR drew 8 email clicks. A smart offer, but it reaches fewer readers than a run everyone can join.

What I'd change next time

  • Lead with something everyone can do. A run, a sale, a gift idea. Broad and dated beat narrow and open-ended this fall.
  • Put a day on it. Black Friday weekend and the holiday runs give every ad a built-in reason to click now.
  • Tell me what came in. If you can share Halloween Run turnout or any new faces who mentioned CHS Happenings, I'll put it next to these numbers.
