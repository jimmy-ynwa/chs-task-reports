The issue went to 11,146 Charleston-area inboxes and 8,063 people opened it. That's a 72.3% open rate. The industry norm for a list like this is 30 to 40%.

Fleet Feet had top billing. Your name sat in the header at the very top of the email, and your Work Place Fit block, with the fitting photo, ran right after the weekend weather and above the lead story. The same “Presented by” card also ran at the top of all five stories on chshappenings.com that week and on the issue's own web page. That added another 1,115 page views, for 9,178 total impressions.

The button opened an email to Wade (Wade@FleetFeetMountPleasant.com). On the website, 4 people tapped it. In the email, I can't count taps on an email link. More on that below.

The honest read

The name got seen. 8,063 local readers opened an issue with Fleet Feet at the very top, and another 1,115 page views on the website carried your card above the story. That's the part of this I can stand behind with numbers.

The part I couldn't measure. Your button was an email link to Wade. That's a fine way to book a fitting, but it means I can't tell you how many people tapped it in the email, which is where almost all the clicks in this newsletter happen. That's on me. I should have given you a web link I could count. The one place I could count taps was the website, and 4 people tapped from the card at the top of the stories. Cards up there usually get read past, so 4 is a real signal.

Wade's inbox is the real scoreboard. The best measure of this one is how many Work Place Fit requests have come in since September 24. If you can get me that number, I'll add it here.

It was a two-step ask. Work Place Fit asks a reader to bring it to their office, which usually means taking it to a boss or HR first. That's a bigger lift than “come see us,” and it tends to show up as fewer, slower responses.

What I'd change for placement 2

Give the button a web page. Point it at a Fleet Feet page, tagged so the visits show up in your own analytics, and keep Wade's email in the copy for anyone ready to book. Every tap gets counted.

Add a one-step ask. If there's an in-store fitting, a group run or an event you'd like to push, that's something a reader can act on alone. Work Place Fit can ride alongside it.
