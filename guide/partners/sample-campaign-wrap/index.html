The honest read

Clicks fell from 11 to 7. That looks worse than it was.

Email clicks to you dropped 30% between the two placements. The whole Sept. 3 issue clicked less, though. Its click rate was 7.68% against 10.16% on Aug. 20, a 24% drop for every link in the newsletter, yours included.

Your share of all clickers barely moved, 1.22% to 1.12%. The creative did not wear out. It got a smaller pie.

What did change was the slot. Aug. 20 had full top billing, with your logo in the header above everything and your block right before the lead story. Sept. 3 was a mid-issue Secondary. That seat is worth about a third of a click per thousand openers: 1.24 against 0.86.

What I'd change next time

  • Tag the link. Both ads pointed at your bare homepage. I counted the clicks, but you can't see those visitors on your end. A tagged link turns 18 clicks into 18 sessions and what each one did next.
  • Keep the Primary slot. Top billing plus a seat before the lead story is where your money goes.
  • Invite, don't filter. Aug. 20 invited readers to see what you could do for their brand. Sept. 3 asked them to find out if they were a fit. The second asks people to rule themselves out before they click.
