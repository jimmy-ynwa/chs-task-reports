What it means for sponsors

Keep leading with the open rate; it's your strongest number. Click-to-open vs. click-to-open is a fair comparison, so "above the news-publisher median" is a claim you can stand behind. Avoid lining it up against raw click rates.

Clicks are where the real race is

Two ways to measure clicks, and mixing them up is the most common mistake in newsletter pitches. Click rate is clicks out of everyone sent. Click-to-open is clicks out of people who opened.

8.5%+
CHS click-to-open

That works out to roughly a 6.1% click rate (72% × 8.5%), which is nearly double beehiiv's 3.23% platform-wide click rate for 2025 and about five times the 1.16% median for media publishers.

The fight isn't opens. It's turning the 72% who open into the 13% who click.

Three things to know before quoting any of these

  • Open rates are inflated everywhere. Apple's privacy feature and inbox scanners trigger "opens" no human made. When GlueLetter strips non-human opens, its news-publisher median drops from 47% to about 16%. beehiiv filters much of this out, which is part of why its numbers read lower than unfiltered sources.
  • Click-to-open is the steady number. Omeda found it barely moved month to month in 2026 while opens drifted. It's the fairest yardstick for comparing CHS with anyone.
  • Platform click rates are falling. beehiiv's dropped from 4.74% to 3.23% in a year as inboxes got more crowded. Holding 8.5%+ click-to-open in that climate is a point in your favor.
