marketchs data refresh: September 2026

Run Saturday, October 10, 2026 · Source: Charleston County MLS (Flexmls)

Bottom line

September's MLS numbers are loaded for all 15 areas and they check out. August was re-pulled and only moved a hair. The one thing still missing is Showings Per Listing, which comes from ShowingTime, not the MLS.

What landed

  • 15 new September 2026 records in Monthly Snapshots: sales, prices, volume, both price ratios, average and median days on market, new listings, pendings, cancellations and expirations, plus median % of original price.
  • 135 new rows in Active Inventory by Price Band (snapshot dated Oct 10), rebuilt from live MLS listing counts. 1793 active listings across the 15 areas.
  • 546 closed sales in September across the 15 areas. Fractional interests and boat slips are left out everywhere.

August, re-pulled

Every August record was refreshed. Eleven areas didn't change at all. Four each picked up one late-reported sale, and the effect was tiny:

  • West Ashley Outside 526: 71 → 72 sales; average days on market 43.5 → 42.9.
  • James Island: 58 → 59 sales; median price $625K → $633K (up); sale-to-original-list 96.4% → 96.3%.
  • Mt Pleasant North: 87 → 88 sales; median price $869K → $867K (down slightly).
  • Mt Pleasant South: 87 → 88 sales; sale-to-original-list 95.0% → 95.1%; average days 30.2 → 30.0.

With only one sale added per area, August's medians weren't re-run; they can't move by more than a rounding step.

Worth knowing

  • West Ashley Outside 526: One September sale was recorded at $21K on a $250K original price, almost certainly a typo. The MLS leaves it out of its price-ratio math, so the median did too.
  • North Charleston Inside 526: The typical home sold for 97.1% of its original price, but the average is 92.2% because a handful of long-sitting homes took deep cuts.
  • Mt Pleasant North: One sale closed at 43% of its original price ($1.4M on $3.25M). The MLS leaves it out of its price-ratio math, so the median did too.
  • Mt Pleasant South: Median sale price ($952.5K) is about 6% above the median list price of the homes that sold ($899K). Buyers paid 97.6% of final list on average, so this is just the mix of homes, not an error.
  • Sullivan's Island: Only 4 sales, and median days on market (67.5) came in above the average (57.5) again. Read Sullivan's on its 12-month window, never one month.
  • Isle of Palms: 13 sales, including one boat slip or fractional share that sold at 67% of original price. It is kept out of the medians, which is why the typical home (93.0% of original price) looks much better than the average (89.2%).
  • Wild Dunes: Only 4 sales, down from 14 in August. The 3-month window smooths this out.
  • Peninsula Inside Crosstown: Pendings fell by half (34 to 17) and 28 listings were cancelled. About half of the listings that ended in September never sold.
  • Peninsula Outside Crosstown: A very fast month: the typical home sold in 2 days.
  • Wando/Cainhoy: A record 54 sales, mostly new construction logged at 0 days on market and full price. A median of 0 days is correct, not a bug.
